# India Geography And History Notes

## Indian Geography

India is located in South Asia and is the seventh-largest country in the world by area. It is bordered by Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar. Sri Lanka and the Maldives are nearby island neighbours in the Indian Ocean region.

India has the Himalayas in the north, the Indo-Gangetic Plain below them, the Thar Desert in the west, the Peninsular Plateau in central and southern India, and long coastal plains along the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.

Major rivers include the Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Indus, Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, and Mahanadi. The Ganga-Brahmaputra basin is one of the most fertile and densely populated regions.

India's climate is strongly influenced by the monsoon. The southwest monsoon brings most rainfall from June to September. The northeast monsoon affects parts of southeastern India, especially Tamil Nadu.

Important mountain ranges include the Himalayas, Aravalli, Vindhya, Satpura, Western Ghats, and Eastern Ghats. The Western Ghats are a biodiversity hotspot.

India has 28 states and 8 union territories. New Delhi is the national capital. Important physical regions include the Himalayan region, Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Indian Desert, Coastal Plains, and Islands.

## Indian History

The Indus Valley Civilization, also called the Harappan Civilization, was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations. Important sites include Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal, Kalibangan, and Rakhigarhi.

The Vedic period saw the composition of the Vedas and the development of early Indo-Aryan society. Later Indian history includes the rise of mahajanapadas and kingdoms such as Magadha.

The Maurya Empire was founded by Chandragupta Maurya. Ashoka, one of its greatest rulers, promoted dhamma and Buddhism after the Kalinga War.

The Gupta period is often described as a classical age of ancient India because of achievements in literature, science, mathematics, astronomy, and art.

Medieval India included regional kingdoms, the Delhi Sultanate, the Vijayanagara Empire, the Bahmani Sultanate, Rajput states, and the Mughal Empire. Akbar is remembered for administrative reforms and policies of religious accommodation.

European trading companies arrived in India from the late 15th century onward. The British East India Company expanded political control after battles such as Plassey in 1757 and Buxar in 1764.

The Revolt of 1857 was a major uprising against British rule. After it, control passed from the East India Company to the British Crown.

The Indian national movement included leaders and organizations such as the Indian National Congress, Mahatma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Nehru, Subhas Chandra Bose, Sardar Patel, Bhagat Singh, B. R. Ambedkar, and many regional movements.

India became independent on 15 August 1947. The Constitution of India came into effect on 26 January 1950, making India a sovereign democratic republic.
